I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [Korteweg-de Vries Institute for Mathematics (KdVI)](https://kdvi.uva.nl/), University of Amsterdam, working with [Tim van Erven](https://www.timvanerven.nl/). My current research focuses on the mathematical foundations of explainable AI. My background is in sequential decision making, particularly bandit algorithms and reinforcement learning, and my broader interests include probability theory, statistical learning, convex analysis, concentration inequalities, and information theory.

I completed my PhD at the [AI&ML group](https://www.upf.edu/web/ai-ml) of Universitat Pompeu Fabra in Barcelona, under the supervision of [Gergely Neu](http://cs.bme.hu/~gergo/).

I have graduated from the [Mathématiques, Vision, Apprentissage](https://www.master-mva.com/) and the [Mathématiques de l'aléatoire](https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/fr/etudiants/masters/mathematiques-et-applications/m2/mathematiques-de-laleatoire/#anchor_0) masters and from the maths department of [École Normale Supérieure Paris](https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/en/welcome/).
